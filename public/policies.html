Privacy policy

This notice explains how the FocusTube application handles data. The site administrator operates this instance and is responsible for its hosting, provider configuration, access to backups, and applicable privacy obligations. Hosting and third-party services may also process information under their own notices.

Information we process

  • Account details: email, verification status, display name, username (required for new accounts), role, account status, and creation date. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, not as plaintext passwords. Password-strength estimates are calculated on your device, without sending passwords to a strength-check service.
  • Learning data: saved course and video metadata, playback positions, progress, notes, tasks, checklists, roadmaps, settings, and recorded site and watch activity.
  • Optional video chat: timed transcripts you load, language and source metadata, questions, completed answers, citations, validated note proposals, selected context, and request/cost records. Saved conversations and proposals are private to their account and included in learning exports. Unsent questions and unconfirmed preview edits remain in page memory; appended content uses the normal notebook save and recovery process.
  • Optional extension capture: the selected video, minimal account ID/name, selected environment, connection state, pending request IDs, and save receipts. The server stores hashes of single-use authorization codes and scoped grant tokens, their parent-session bindings, expiry, and revocation information. The extension does not copy full learning profiles, page contents, or background browsing history.
  • Feedback: report and reply text, chosen visibility, optional screenshots and reproduction/device details, account references, and moderation/status decisions. Feedback does not automatically capture the screen or collect notes, full page URLs, console logs, or session contents. You select screenshots explicitly. The server validates and re-encodes them as PNGs, removes original metadata, and may reduce dimensions. Original filenames are not stored.
  • Security and activity information: hashed session tokens, hashed verification challenges and invitation secrets, invitation expiry/use/revocation metadata, rate-limit records, limited account events, and coarse activity status. Source addresses and identifiers are used to enforce security limits; authentication rate-limit keys are HMAC-derived.
  • Operational information: request counts, response codes, timing, service health, and bounded diagnostic logs. Application logs exclude raw request bodies, cookies, password/code values, email addresses, and note content. Hosting or proxy logs can differ.

Why information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, send verification messages, operate and restore your workspace, display learning statistics, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot failures. The application does not implement advertising profiles, session replay, or recording of typed keystrokes. Cursor reflection coordinates are used only to render the effect on your device and are not sent to the server by that feature.

Cookies and device storage

An HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict session cookie maintains sign-in. Session lifetime is configured by the administrator, with a default of 30 days. Signing out invalidates that session. Changing your password revokes old sessions and creates a replacement session for the browser completing the change.

Browser storage holds preferences such as theme, appearance, course-panel visibility, and notes dimensions. It may also hold unsynchronized note drafts for recovery or earlier browser-only course data. Clearing browser storage removes these preferences and may remove unsaved drafts. Device-only preferences are not included in server data exports.

The Chrome extension first tries credentialed requests using your existing session in that browser profile, without reading the HttpOnly cookie. If browser policy blocks reuse, Connect FocusTube opens the selected site's sign-in and explicit consent flow using PKCE and a single-use code. Passwords are entered on the FocusTube site, not in the extension. Its permissions are context menus, the active tab, storage, identity for this connection flow, and optional access to the selected FocusTube host; it does not request cookies, browsing history, scripting, or all-sites access. Hosted connections use HTTPS. The temporary Local testing option uses HTTP only on 127.0.0.1 port 3110 on your device, with a separate local account and an explicitly enabled loopback-only server. Browser permission covers the loopback host, while code and CSP restrict the port; local testing does not send a save to either hosted environment.

A fallback opaque grant is stored in chrome.storage.local with access limited to trusted extension contexts, not in browser sync. This is browser-profile storage, not an encrypted secret vault. Temporary PKCE verifier/state and popup details use chrome.storage.session. The grant can only identify the member, save a video, or disconnect its own connection; it does not authorize reading notebooks, full profiles, exports, or administration.

Who can access information

Members can access their own learning records through the application. Active administrators can view limited account and usage metadata for administration; that role does not itself grant application access to other members' private notes or courses. People operating the server or maintaining its database and backups may have infrastructure-level access and must protect it appropriately.

Active administrators can access private feedback reports, replies, and screenshots, while other testers cannot. Screenshot URLs recheck the current viewer's permission; hidden-reply screenshots are administrator-only. Images are stored in the application's database and backups, not on a public upload directory or external image service. Public feedback displays usernames or a generic author name, not account emails or full display names. The first forum version does not send feedback email notifications or publish reports to external issue trackers. Existing account verification mail is separate.

The configured email provider processes recipient addresses and verification messages. Loading YouTube scripts, videos, or thumbnails can send browser and network information to Google/YouTube. The player first uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced host but may fall back to the standard embed host; this is not a guarantee of zero third-party tracking. See Google's privacy policy.

When video chat is enabled and you consent, the FocusTube server uses its Google API key to send Google Gemini your question, video title, available captured playback position, and selected caption/conversation context. Current moment uses caption segments overlapping 120 seconds before to 60 seconds after that position; Whole video uses the full permitted transcript within the input limit. Normal follow-ups include the last four completed exchanges. Discussion summaries include the chosen completed discussion and its referenced captions, not just the last four exchanges. Oversized context is rejected rather than silently shortened.

Existing notebook contents, account credentials, and other videos are not automatically included in model requests. Anything you enter into a question is sent, so do not include secrets or material you lack permission to share. Optional caption retrieval separately contacts YouTube, and extension previews may load the selected video's thumbnail from YouTube. Provider data use and retention depend on the administrator's account and Google's Gemini API terms; local storage does not mean inference runs on this server. Application chat handlers do not log prompt, transcript, or answer text. Clearing local chat does not itself request deletion from Google.

Cloudflare Turnstile is contacted only when the administrator enables CAPTCHA. It processes challenge information and the source address to check for abuse; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Optional external monitoring may receive sanitized operational logs and metrics when enabled. Ask the administrator which hosting, mail, security, and monitoring providers are used by this instance and where they process data.

Retention

Email codes expire after ten minutes and allow at most five incorrect guesses. Session and security records are subject to configured expiry, rate limits, and periodic cleanup. Account-event history retains up to 30 days and is capped at 10,000 records; daily membership aggregates retain up to 90 days. Old last-sign-in/activity timestamps are cleared by periodic cleanup after 30 days. Diagnostic logs use bounded rotation rather than a guaranteed fixed number of days.

New member invitations default to seven days; administrators can select a shorter or longer future expiry within 365 days, change an eligible link's expiry, or revoke it. Existing expiry dates are not changed automatically. Expired links with unused slots require explicit reactivation; revoked or exhausted links cannot be revived. The first-administrator bootstrap stays one-use/24-hour. Invitation records are eligible for cleanup 30 days after expiry; the stored hash cannot reconstruct a lost invitation link.

Regular account and learning data have no automatic age-based deletion in the application. Removing a course preserves its notebook. Deleting a notebook clears its content while retaining revision markers to prevent stale tabs from restoring it. Existing guest profiles are removed after 90 days of inactivity. Backups can retain earlier data after live records are deleted, until the administrator removes or expires those backups.

Video chats and transcripts remain until cleared, replaced, or removed with the account. Removing a course alone preserves its stored chat for export. Clear chat removes conversation text and proposals; Remove transcript also removes captions. Already appended notebook text is separate. A spending ledger retains request identifiers, fingerprints, dates, and costs to enforce the budget shared by accounts in that instance, including after chat clearing or a learning-backup restore; it contains no transcript or conversation text. Separate environments do not share that ledger. Dispatched requests with unknown final usage, including cancellation, retain their maximum reservation rather than assuming a refund. Account removal detaches that ledger from the account. Older operator backups may still contain removed content.

Extension authorization codes are single-use and valid for at most five minutes. Grants expire within 30 days and never later than their parent session. Logout, password or email session rotation, browser account/session replacement, disabled/deleted accounts, parent expiry, and successful Disconnect invalidate the affected connection. A cookie/grant mismatch stops saving and requires reconnection. An offline Disconnect pauses saves but leaves server revocation unconfirmed until retried; removing the extension or its local storage alone does not confirm server revocation.

The extension retains up to 20 pending operations across accounts/environments for 24 hours, pruning expired entries on subsequent use. Retry and Discard do not transfer an operation to another account. Server save receipts are eligible for bounded cleanup after 30 days, with caps of 10,000 per account and 100,000 per database; physical deletion is not an exact deadline. A discarded or expired pending item does not undo a save that may already have reached the server. Successfully saved videos remain ordinary library records until you remove them; operator backups can retain earlier receipt and security records.

Feedback reports, replies, and screenshots also have no automatic age-based deletion. Hiding content is moderation, not erasure, and cannot recall copies someone already viewed or downloaded. Shared feedback is separate from learning exports/imports. Contact the operator for feedback access, correction, erasure, or backup-retention requests. Unsent feedback drafts and selected screenshots are held only in page memory and are cleared on account changes or page exit. Temporary preview URLs are revoked when the page is hidden or its viewer changes.
