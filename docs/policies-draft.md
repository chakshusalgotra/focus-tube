# FocusTube Policy Drafts

Status: historical working draft and operator review checklist. Basic terms and privacy notices were published on 15 September 2026 in [public/policies.html](../public/policies.html), linked from Settings and sign-in. The page supports signed-in password changes; it does not provide forgotten-password recovery or account deletion. This document is not the published policy, legal advice, or a compliance certification. The deployment-specific details below still need operator/legal review and should be reflected in the published notices when supplied.

## Information Required Before Publication

- Operator's legal name, location, and contact address.
- Privacy/support contact and a process for access, correction, and deletion requests.
- Intended audience, any age restrictions, and relevant jurisdictions.
- Actual hosting, email, CAPTCHA, and monitoring providers, processing regions, and transfer safeguards where required.
- Approved retention/deletion rules for account data, learning records, database backups, and external provider logs.
- Effective date and the process for announcing material policy changes.
- Any legal basis, statutory disclosures, contractual limitations, or additional terms required for the intended deployment.

## Draft Terms of Use

### About the Service

FocusTube provides an invitation-only learning workspace for organizing public YouTube courses, tracking progress, writing notes, and managing learning tasks. Service access is provided by the operator identified in the published version of these terms. Availability and third-party video access can change.

### Accounts

Use an invitation intended for you and account details you are authorized to provide. Keep passwords, verification codes, invitation links, and account exports private. Notify the operator about suspected unauthorized access. Usernames must be unique within this instance; changing a username changes the username used for future sign-ins, not the ownership of saved learning data.

### Acceptable Use

Do not bypass access controls, use another person's account without permission, interfere with the service, or upload content you are not entitled to use. Respect applicable laws and third-party platform terms. Access restrictions and any appeal process must be specified by the operator before these terms are published.

### Content and Third Parties

You retain your rights in notes and other original content you provide. The service processes that content to operate your workspace. YouTube content remains subject to the rights of its owners and YouTube's terms. FocusTube does not grant a license to redistribute third-party videos. A video may become unavailable or disallow embedding independently of FocusTube.

### Data and Backups

You can export your workspace data from Settings. Importing a backup replaces the current workspace's courses, progress, notes, settings, and history after confirmation; account credentials are not replaced. Keep exports secure. A certificate generated by FocusTube records application completion and is not a representation of accreditation by the operator or video creator.

### Contact and Changes

The published terms must provide the operator's contact details, effective date, account closure process, and a notice procedure for material changes. No governing-law, liability, payment, or dispute-resolution provisions are asserted in this draft; these require operator and legal review.

## Draft Privacy Notice

### Information Processed

- Account information: email address, verification status, display name, optional username, role, creation date, and account status. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes. Session tokens and verification challenges are stored as hashes rather than raw codes/tokens.
- Learning information: saved course and video metadata, progress and playback positions, notes, tasks, checklists, roadmaps, and recorded learning/watch activity.
- Account activity: coarse activity status, last recorded sign-in/activity, and limited account events available to active administrators. Application administrator access does not itself grant access to other members' private learning content; operators with database/backup access have infrastructure-level access.
- Operational information: bounded request counts, response codes, timing, application health, and sanitized logs. Application logs exclude raw request bodies, cookies, verification secrets, email addresses, and note/course content. Hosting or proxy logs may differ and must be disclosed for the actual deployment.
- Security checks: source IP addresses and account/email identifiers are used to enforce limits; stored rate-limit keys are HMAC-derived. CAPTCHA, when configured, sends the challenge and source address to its provider for verification.

### Purposes

Information is used to authenticate accounts, deliver verification messages, operate and restore the learning workspace, display personal statistics, prevent abuse, and diagnose service failures. The published notice must identify the applicable legal bases rather than assume that use of the service constitutes consent to every form of processing.

### Cookies and Browser Storage

An HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict session cookie maintains sign-in. Its lifetime defaults to 30 days but is configurable; the published notice must state the deployed value. Browser storage holds theme, course-layout, appearance and notes-size preferences, and may retain local note drafts for recovery. Some legacy browser-only course data may remain until handled by the user. Clearing browser storage removes device preferences and may remove unsynchronized drafts. Cursor reflection coordinates are used only for on-device rendering and are not sent to the server by this feature.

### External Services

Loading YouTube scripts, embedded videos, or thumbnails can communicate browser/network information to Google/YouTube. The player first uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced host but can fall back to the standard embed host; this is not a guarantee of zero third-party tracking. Opening a source video leaves FocusTube.

The configured email provider processes recipient addresses and verification messages. Cloudflare Turnstile is contacted only when CAPTCHA is configured. Optional external monitoring is disabled by default and must be disclosed before it is activated. Identify actual providers and link their applicable notices before publication. Do not claim that a provider's acceptance of an email proves inbox delivery.

### Retention and Access

Email verification challenges expire after ten minutes and permit at most five incorrect guesses. Rate limits and periodic cleanup limit retained security records. Account audit events retain up to 30 days, capped at 10,000 records; daily membership aggregates retain up to 90 days. Old last-sign-in/activity timestamps are cleared by periodic cleanup after 30 days. File and Docker logs use bounded rotation, not a guaranteed fixed number of days.

Regular account and learning data do not currently have an automatic age-based deletion policy. Removing a course preserves its notebook; deleting a notebook clears note content while retaining revision markers to prevent stale tabs from restoring it. Existing guest profiles are removed after 90 days of inactivity. Database backups can retain earlier data beyond live-record deletion; the operator must define backup retention and deletion procedures before publication.

Users can export their learning data and correct supported account details in Settings. There is no self-service account deletion or arbitrary email-change workflow in this release. The published notice must provide a working operator contact for other requests and state applicable rights and response procedures. It must not advertise controls or guarantees that the deployed service does not provide.

### Security and Updates

Access controls, hashed credentials, encrypted SMTP transport, and production HTTPS configuration reduce risk; they do not guarantee that any service is perfectly secure. The operator is responsible for deployment configuration, access to databases/backups, provider arrangements, and legally required notices. Publish reviewed policy versions with an effective date and inform users of material changes through the operator's approved process.
